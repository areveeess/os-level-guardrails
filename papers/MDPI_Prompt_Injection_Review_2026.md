# Prompt Injection Attacks in LLMs and AI Agent Systems: A Comprehensive Review

**Journal:** Information (MDPI), Volume 17, Issue 1, Article 54 (January 2026)
**Authors:** S. Gulyamov et al.

**Summary:**
This systematic review synthesizes findings from 45 key sources on prompt injection in LLMs and autonomous AI agent systems. It highlights how these attacks act as the "SQL injection of the AI era," exploiting the inability of models to distinguish instructions from untrusted data. 

The paper proposes the **PALADIN five-layer defense framework** to mitigate direct, indirect, and memory-poisoning injections.
